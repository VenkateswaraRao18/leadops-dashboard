## Company Overview
We are AgenTech, a B2B SaaS company that builds AI-powered automation tools for enterprise sales teams.
Our platform helps companies automate lead qualification, onboarding, and customer follow-up using AI agents.
Founded in 2023, we serve over 500 enterprise customers globally.

## Pricing
- Starter Plan: $99/month — up to 5 users, 500 leads/month, basic reporting
- Growth Plan: $299/month — up to 25 users, 5000 leads/month, advanced analytics, CRM integrations
- Enterprise Plan: $999/month — unlimited users, unlimited leads, custom integrations, dedicated support, SLA guarantee
- Annual discount: 20% off all plans when billed annually
- Minimum contract for Enterprise: 12 months
- Custom pricing available for 100+ seat deals — contact sales@agentech.ai

## Product Features
- AI lead scoring and qualification
- Automated onboarding workflows
- CRM integrations (HubSpot, Salesforce, Attio)
- Calendar booking automation (Google Calendar, Calendly)
- Email automation (personalized sequences)
- RAG-powered knowledge base for support agents
- Real-time analytics dashboard
- Compliance: SOC2 Type II certified, GDPR compliant

## Onboarding Process
1. Account setup and CRM integration (Day 1)
2. Knowledge base ingestion — upload your docs, FAQs, pricing (Day 1-2)
3. Agent configuration and prompt tuning (Day 2-3)
4. Test runs with synthetic leads (Day 3-4)
5. Go-live with human approval gates enabled (Day 5)
6. Dedicated CSM assigned for first 90 days
